Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers the Neqra Apps publishing tool, which Neqra Apps uses to publish its own posts to its own social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram and Telegram).

What the tool does

The tool runs on a computer operated by Neqra Apps. At scheduled times it uploads images and captions created by Neqra Apps to the Neqra Apps Facebook Page, Instagram account and Telegram channel.

Information we collect

The tool does not collect, store or sell personal information about other people. It does not read messages, comments, followers or profiles. It only uses the access tokens of Neqra Apps' own accounts, which are stored privately on our computer, and the names and IDs of those accounts.

Images

To let Instagram fetch a post image, the image is placed in a storage bucket with a public link. These images are Neqra Apps' own promotional posters and contain no personal data.

Sharing

Content is shared only with the platform it is posted to (Meta or Telegram), under that platform's own terms and privacy policy.

Data deletion

The tool holds no data about you. If you believe we hold information about you, or you want any data removed, message us at t.me/Neqrasupportbot with the words "Delete my data". We will reply and complete the request within 30 days.
